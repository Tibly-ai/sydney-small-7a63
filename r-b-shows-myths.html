By Sydney Small · Updated October 18, 2026

Here's what buyers usually get wrong before they book.

Myth: You need a full band with horns to make R&B sound authentic

Reality: Authenticity comes from the vocal and the arrangement, not headcount. Sydney Small performs R&B, soul, pop, jazz, and rock classics backed by a dynamic live band, and that same material scales down to a duo or solo set with backing tracks when the room, budget, or event format calls for it. A stripped-down arrangement can hit just as hard as a full band if the vocalist knows how to carry the song.

Myth: Live R&B shows only work in big concert venues

Reality: Sydney Small has performed R&B and soul sets at some of New York City's most respected live music venues, including The Groove, the W Hotel, the Rainbow Room, Café Wha?, The Flatiron Room (NoMad and Murray Hill), Prohibition, The Red Lion, and Casa Cipriani — rooms that range from intimate basements to elegant private spaces, not just large stages. The same catalog that works in a 200-seat room also works as a 45 to 90 minute set at a private event.

Myth: An R&B vocalist can't also deliver pop, soul, and jazz convincingly

Reality: Genre crossover is the norm for a working vocalist, not the exception. Sydney Small is a soul and jazz vocalist whose sets move between pop, R&B, soul, jazz, and rock classics in the same night, drawing on influences like Amy Winehouse, Adele, and Aretha Franklin. A singer who's spent years holding residencies across multiple rooms has already had to prove range, not just one lane.

Myth: Booking live R&B means committing to a massive, unpredictable budget

Reality: Pricing is built around what the specific event needs — set length, band size, and location — not a flat rate tied to the genre. There's no requirement to book the biggest configuration to get a real R&B performance.

Myth: R&B sets are too loud or too energetic for smaller private events

Reality: Set energy adjusts to the room. A wedding cocktail hour or a quieter private dinner calls for a different arrangement than a late-night club set, and a solo or duo configuration can deliver the same R&B and soul repertoire at a volume and intensity that fits a smaller or more intimate crowd.

Myth: Live R&B performers only cover current chart hits

Reality: Classic soul and R&B material is core to this catalog, not an afterthought. Sydney Small blends the timeless richness of classic soul with a modern, heartfelt edge, with a sound shaped by powerhouse voices like Aretha Franklin alongside contemporary pop and R&B influences. Myth: You have to book months in advance to get a real band, not a backing-track act
