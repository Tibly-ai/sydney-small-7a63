What to Decide Before You Call

Before reaching out, it helps to have a few basics worked out in your head:

  • The date. Even a rough window helps if your date isn't locked yet.
  • The venue or address. This affects logistics like sound and setup.
  • The event type. Private party, corporate event, venue booking, or something else.
  • The format you want. Full band, duo, or solo with backing tracks — whatever fits the room.
  • Your budget range. Sydney prices each booking based on what the room actually needs, so having a number in mind (even a range) speeds up the conversation.

What to Have Ready for the First Conversation

The booking page asks for five simple things: your name, email, event date, venue or address, and event type, plus a note on your budget and any details about the event. Having these ready before you fill out the form (or call) means you'll get a faster, more accurate response. If you already know you want certain songs or artists referenced in the set, mention that too — it helps shape the plan Sydney sends back.

What the First Conversation Covers

Once you send a booking request, Sydney reads every request herself and writes back within 48 hours with availability and a plan. What the Estimate Will Include

Because every room is different, pricing is built around what the event actually needs rather than a flat rate. A few things that shape the scope of any quote:

  • Format — full band, duo, or solo with backing tracks.
  • Set length — most bookings run 45 to 90 minutes of live singing.
  • Sound — Sydney brings a PA if your venue needs one.
  • Travel — travel outside NYC's five boroughs is billed separately.

Rather than guess at numbers, the most accurate way to get a scope is to send your date, venue, and budget through the booking page and let Sydney build a plan around your specific event.
