What Affects Your Price

Format — solo, duo, or full band. A solo performance with backing tracks costs less than a duo, and a duo costs less than the full four-piece band setup. The right choice depends on your guest count and the size of your venue.

Set length and structure. Most bookings run 45 to 90 minutes of live singing. Whether you want one continuous set during the reception or music spread across the ceremony, cocktail hour, and first dance changes the scope — and the price.

Sound and production needs. If your venue doesn't have its own PA system, Sydney brings one — which is an added line item versus a venue that's already wired for sound.

Venue and market. Pricing reflects what the room actually needs. A Manhattan ballroom, a Brooklyn loft, and a backyard wedding in New Jersey don't require the same production, and rates are set accordingly for each of Sydney's markets: New York, NY; Brooklyn; New Jersey; Nashville; Miami; and Palm Beach, FL.

Song requests and learning custom material. For weddings, Sydney learns your song for the first dance or ceremony moment — a customization that's built into the wedding booking process rather than priced as a rigid add-on.
