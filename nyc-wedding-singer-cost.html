What Affects Your Price

A few things move the number up or down more than anything else:

  • Format. A full four-piece band costs more to book than a solo or duo set — that's the single biggest lever in your price.
  • Run time. Bookings are built around 45–90 minutes of live singing. Anything longer than that window is priced as an add-on.
  • Event type. Weddings (ceremony, cocktail hour, first dance), corporate parties, private parties, and venue headliner/support slots are each priced based on what the room needs — not a flat rate across categories.
  • Custom song learning. Sydney learns your song for first dances and ceremony moments; this is part of how the wedding package is scoped.
  • Date and season. Sydney is now booking Fall 2026 / Early 2027 dates — availability during peak wedding season affects pricing and lead time.
  • Market. Bookings across New York, NY; Nashville; New Jersey; Brooklyn; Miami; and Palm Beach, FL may carry different logistics depending on distance and venue.
