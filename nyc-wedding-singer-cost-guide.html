What Affects Your Price

  • Format — Solo with backing tracks, duo, or full four-piece band each carry different costs, since a full band means more musicians, more gear, and more setup time.
  • Event type and portion of the day — Ceremony-only, cocktail hour, first dance, or a full reception set are priced differently depending on how much live singing time you need.
  • Set length — Most bookings run 45 to 90 minutes of live singing; longer sets or multiple sets across a night affect the total.
  • Sound equipment needs — A PA is provided if your venue doesn't already have one, which factors into the quote.
  • Song learning requests — Learning a specific first-dance song or a personal request is built into the booking process ("I learn your song").
  • Venue and location — New York City, Brooklyn, New Jersey, and other markets Sydney books in may have different logistics (parking, load-in, travel) that affect price.
  • Date and season — Sydney is now booking Fall 2026 / Early 2027 dates; peak wedding dates may affect availability and pricing.

How Booking Works

There's no public price list because every wedding is quoted individually. Instead, couples send the date, the venue or address, the type of event, and their budget, and receive a reply within 48 hours with availability and a plan. This lets pricing reflect exactly what the room and the couple need — full band vs. stripped-down, one set vs. multiple, ceremony vs. reception — rather than forcing every wedding into the same package.
