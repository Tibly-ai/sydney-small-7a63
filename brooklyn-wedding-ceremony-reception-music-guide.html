How to Structure Live Music Across Your Wedding Day

A typical Brooklyn wedding transitions through three distinct musical environments over the course of the day. Coordinating these moments through a single music provider eliminates hand-offs between separate vendors and maintains a cohesive tone throughout the event.

Event Segment Recommended Format Performance Focus Key Benefit
Ceremony Solo or Duo (Acoustic/Tracks) Processional, vows, recessional; custom song learning Intimate, clear audio focused on the couple's chosen moments
Cocktail Hour Duo or Stripped-Down Set Easy live pop, soul, and R&B hits while guests mingle Keeps energy up while allowing guest conversation
Reception Full 4-Piece Live Band 45 to 90 minutes of high-energy pop classics and dance hits Fills the dance floor with a cohesive live concert atmosphere

1. The Ceremony

The ceremony relies on precision and vocal clarity. Whether performing for a traditional aisle walk or an intimate vow exchange, Sydney learns your specific song request to personalize the processional and recessional moments.

2. The Cocktail Hour

As guests transition to drinks and light bites, the music shifts to easy live pop and classic soul. The goal of this segment is to establish an upbeat, welcoming atmosphere without competing with guest conversations.

3. The Reception

The reception requires a powerful live presence to open the dance floor. Fronting her four-piece band, Sydney performs 45 to 90-minute sets packed with pop, R&B, and soul hits inspired by powerhouse vocalists like Amy Winehouse, Adele, and Aretha Franklin.
