What Affects Your Price

  • Band size — Solo, duo, and full four-piece band bookings are priced differently, since more musicians means more cost.
  • Set length — Most events run 45–90 minutes of live singing; longer sets or multiple sets (ceremony + cocktail hour + reception, for example) affect the quote.
  • Sound needs — Whether the venue already has a PA or Sydney needs to bring one changes the price.
  • Event type — Weddings, corporate holiday parties, private birthdays, and venue headliner bookings each have different scopes (song learning for a first dance, clean on-time sets for corporate, full or stripped-down band for private parties).
  • Location — Bookings in New York, Nashville, New Jersey, Brooklyn, Miami, and Palm Beach may carry different travel considerations.
  • Timing/season — Sydney is currently booking Fall 2026 / Early 2027, and peak dates may affect availability and pricing.
