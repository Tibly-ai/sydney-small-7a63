What to Check Before Booking Any Live Music Provider

Whether you're comparing Sydney Small, Hank Lane Music, or anyone else, run every provider through the same checklist:

  • Licensing and insurance — Does the performer or agency carry the coverage your venue requires?
  • Written estimates — Do you get a real number tied to your date, headcount, and format, or a vague "starting at" quote?
  • Format flexibility — Can they scale from an intimate ceremony to a full dance-floor reception without changing the sound or repertoire?
  • Verifiable performance history — Can you independently confirm they've played real rooms on real dates, not just private event photos?
  • Communication and response time — Who answers your booking request, how fast, and do you get a direct line to the performer or a layer of agency staff?
  • Local and multi-market experience — Have they actually worked your city, or are they generalists claiming broad coverage?
