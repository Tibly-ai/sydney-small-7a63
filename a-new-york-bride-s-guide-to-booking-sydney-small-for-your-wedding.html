A New York Bride's Guide to Booking Sydney Small for Your Wedding

By Sydney Small · Updated October 11, 2026

Sydney Small is a New York City–based soul and pop vocalist who sings live for weddings — ceremony, cocktail hour, and reception — as a full band, duo, or solo act, built around the songs your people actually want to hear. You send her your date, venue, and budget; she replies within 48 hours with availability and a plan. No templated packages, no middleman — she reads every request herself.

If you're the bride planning this, you're likely juggling a planner, a venue coordinator, a partner with opinions on the first dance, and a budget that has to stretch across a dozen vendors. Here's what to know before you reach out.

What a New York Bride Can Expect from the Performance

Sydney's wedding sets are built in three parts, and you can book one, two, or all three depending on your day:

  • Ceremony — processional, vows, and recessional. She learns your specific song rather than substituting a stock arrangement.
  • Cocktail Hour — easy live pop while guests find their drinks and settle in.
  • Reception — 45 to 90 minutes of hits aimed at keeping the dance floor full.

She's been fronting a live band around New York since 2018, backed by a dynamic live band for the bigger sets, and she's performed at New York rooms including The Groove, the W Hotel, the Rainbow Room, Café Wha?, The Flatiron Room (NoMad and Murray Hill), Prohibition, The Red Lion, and Casa Cipriani — so she's used to reading a room, not just a setlist.

What to Ask For — Song Choices, Set Length, and Sound

A few things worth putting in your first email so the plan Sydney sends back is useful right away:

  • Your must-play songs, especially the processional, first dance, and any song with personal meaning — she learns the specific song rather than offering a fixed list to choose from.
  • Set length expectations. Most bookings run 45 to 90 minutes of live singing for the reception portion; decide with your planner where that fits your timeline.
  • PA and sound needs. She brings a PA if your venue needs one — tell her early whether your venue has house sound or not.
  • Travel. If your venue is outside NYC's five boroughs, travel is billed separately, so flag your location up front.
