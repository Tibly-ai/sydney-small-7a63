How Much Does It Cost to Hire a Professional Wedding Singer in New York?

The cost of a wedding singer in New York comes down to five variables. Change any one, and the quote changes with it:

  • Format — A full four-piece band, a duo, or a solo performance with backing tracks. Sydney offers all three, and each carries a different footprint and cost to coordinate.
  • Length of the booking — Most bookings run 45 to 90 minutes of live singing. A single continuous reception set is a different scope than music spread across ceremony, cocktail hour, dinner, toasts, and dancing.
  • Sound needs — If your venue doesn't already have a PA system, Sydney brings one, which factors into the quote. A venue with in-house sound handled can come in lighter.
  • Learned or custom songs — Learning a couple's first-dance song or a specific ceremony request is part of the booking and factors into scope.
  • Date and location — Sydney is now booking Fall 2026 / Early 2027 across New York, NY; Brooklyn; New Jersey; Nashville; Miami; and Palm Beach, FL. Travel outside NYC's five boroughs is billed separately, and peak dates can affect availability.
