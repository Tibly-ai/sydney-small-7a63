What Affects Your Price

Several factors change the cost of booking a live performer for your NYC event:

  • Format — Sydney offers a full band, a duo, or a solo performance with backing tracks. A four-piece band costs more than a solo or duo setup because more musicians are being paid and coordinated.
  • Length of the set — Most bookings run 45 to 90 minutes of live singing. A single continuous set is priced differently than music broken up across dinner, toasts, and dancing, since that usually means a longer overall booking window.
  • Sound needs — If your venue doesn't already have its own PA system, Sydney brings one, which is factored into the quote. Venues with in-house sound may cost less to book.
  • Event type — Weddings (ceremony, cocktail hour, first dance), corporate events (holiday parties, launches, client dinners), private parties (birthdays, engagements, backyard shows), and venue bookings (headliner or support slot) each have different scopes and are priced individually.
  • Learned or custom songs — For weddings specifically, learning your chosen song for a first dance or ceremony moment is part of the booking and factors into scope.
  • Date and market — Sydney is now booking Fall 2026 / Early 2027 across New York, NY, Brooklyn, New Jersey, Nashville, Miami, and Palm Beach, FL. Peak dates and travel outside NYC can affect price.
