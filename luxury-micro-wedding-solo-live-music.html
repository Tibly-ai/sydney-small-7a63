Why Couples Choose Sydney for a Micro Wedding

Sydney's career began performing at private weddings, high-end events, and exclusive shows for celebrity audiences, originally in Los Angeles, before she moved to New York and built a name fronting a four-piece band around the city. That background — reading a room, learning the songs a client actually wants, and delivering a clean, professional set — is what she brings to luxury micro wedding bookings, whether the ceremony is a backyard, a rooftop, a hotel suite, or a private residence.

She has performed at some of New York City's most respected live music venues, including The Groove, the W Hotel, the Rainbow Room, Café Wha?, The Flatiron Room (NoMad and Murray Hill), Prohibition, The Red Lion, and Casa Cipriani — a résumé that translates directly to the polish couples expect at a high-end, intimate wedding.

What's Included in the Solo Micro Wedding Format

For a luxury micro wedding, a full band isn't always the right footprint — but recorded music isn't the moment either. Sydney's solo format is built for exactly that gap:

  • Solo with backing tracks — a full, polished sound in a small footprint, built for intimate ceremonies, garden spaces, and smaller reception rooms.
  • Duo — stripped-down but live, for ceremonies and cocktail hours that want more texture than a solo voice and track.
  • Custom song learning — Sydney learns your ceremony song, first dance, or a specific request rather than working from a generic playlist.
  • PA provided if your venue needs one.
  • Most bookings run 45 to 90 minutes of live singing.
