"I didn't grow up wanting to be background music. I grew up wanting the whole room to sing along."

Sydney Small has spent six years playing every kind of New York room, from folding-chair basements on the Lower East Side to warehouse floors in Ridgewood. The set is pop, all the way through: the songs people already know the words to, sung loud and a little rough around the edges.

No backing tracks, no polish for polish's sake. Just a full band, a fog machine that works about half the time, and a crowd that ends up hoarse by the encore.
