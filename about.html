Sydney Small has spent countless years turning small rooms in New York City into full-on singalongs, one Franklin note and one King chorus at a time.

These days, Sydney performs at some of New York City's most respected live music venues, including The Groove, the W Hotel, the Rainbow Room, Café Wha?, The Flatiron Room (NoMad and Murray Hill, Prohibition, The Red Lion, and Casa Cipriani. Backed by a dynamic live band, she delivers soulful, high-energy performances of the pop, R&B, soul, jazz, and rock classics audiences know and love—creating unforgettable nights built around timeless songs and genuine musicianship.
