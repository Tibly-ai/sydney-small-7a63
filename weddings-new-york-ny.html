Booking one performer who can handle all three, at the scale your venue actually calls for, is the difference between a day that flows and a day that feels stitched together from separate vendors.

What a New York Wedding Booking Includes

  • Ceremony — Processional, vows, recessional. Song learning is built into the booking, so your specific processional or recessional request gets prepared ahead of time.
  • Cocktail Hour — Live pop that sits under conversation while guests settle in, typically handled by a solo or duo format even when a full band is booked for the reception.
  • Reception — 45 to 90 minutes of live singing built to keep the floor full, whether delivered as one continuous set or broken across dinner, toasts, and dancing.
  • Format flexibility — Full band, duo, or solo with backing tracks, chosen based on your venue size and guest count. Same repertoire and voice at every scale.
  • Sound — If your New York venue doesn't already have a PA system, Sydney brings one; confirm your venue's existing setup first so you're not paying for gear you don't need.

How Pricing Works for a New York Wedding

There's no fixed rate card. Every New York wedding is priced on what the room actually needs — a Manhattan ceremony with a duo and backing tracks is a different booking than a full four-piece band for a large reception. Note that travel outside NYC's five boroughs is billed separately, which is worth flagging if your ceremony and reception are in different parts of the metro area.
